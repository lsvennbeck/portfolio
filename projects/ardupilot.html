Misson planner


Redesign of Ardupilot Mission planner(WIP). Mission planner is an open-source drone flight and mission planning software.

Here are some of the improvements:

Separated actions and data so that they can be viewed simultaneously, as users often switch between them during flight.

Enabled having multiple data windows open simultaneously.

Clearer grouping of related action buttons.

Improved video stream visibility by removing flight display information that was previously overlaid.
