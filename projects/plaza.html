Crowne Plaza
+ IDEO

Role: Design lead digital, UX/UI
Team: Will Barker, Jenny Winfield, Miha Feus, Lars Svennbeck

Ignition is a new meeting and working experience, housed in the lobby of Crowne Plaza hotels that incorporates a new suite of brand, space, furniture, service role, food and beverage offers, digital tools, and business strategy.

The concept is designed to increase travelers productivity and launched with two pilots in Atlanta.

Huddle spot with integrated iPad and whiteboard wall


Ignition has everything from single-person Pods, to the Nooks and Huddles for four-person impromptu meetings, to the 10-person Apartment (bookable by the hour).
